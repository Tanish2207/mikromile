04
One hard design rule

Commerce never depends
on kindness.

✓

A group buy completes on its own — collect at the community pickup point, zero errands required.

✓

Errands are optional piggybacking → "I'm collecting the order at 5 PM, I'll drop your share at the hostel gate."

✕

Never: a pooled order stuck waiting for a random stranger to accept a delivery.

05
The trust machine

Pledge → Pay → Collect.
Or get refunded.

No participant can lose money on a half-formed deal. If the minimum threshold is missed, the deal auto-cancels and everyone is refunded. Safety is an architectural default, not a policy.

Why so lean
  • The prototype runs at ≈ ₹0 today — free tiers, localStorage, OpenStreetMap
